About Union Assurance

Protecting Sri Lanka for Over Three Decades

Founded in 1987 and headquartered in Colombo, Union Assurance PLC is a member of the prestigious John Keells Holdings group — one of Sri Lanka's largest diversified conglomerates. We are a leading life insurance provider regulated by the Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL).

Our mission is to make world-class insurance accessible to every Sri Lankan family and business — backed by financial strength, technological innovation, and an unwavering commitment to customer care.
